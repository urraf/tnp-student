FAQ

Questions students ask

Is this the official TnP app?

No. TnP Student is an unofficial, open-source app built by a student. It uses your existing TnP portal account and the same portal as student.tnpnsut.in, so everything you do in the app is visible on the website too.

Is it safe to sign in?

Your email and password are sent directly to the official TnP server over HTTPS — never to anyone else. The source code is public, so you (or a friend) can verify exactly what it does.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

It's distributed as a free APK from GitHub to keep it simple and open. Android will ask you to allow installing it once; after that, updates arrive inside the app.

Alerts only show up when I open the app — what do I do?

Your phone is saving battery by pausing the app. Open More → Notifications and tap the background-checks row, then allow background activity. On Xiaomi, Redmi, Poco, Oppo, Realme, Vivo and OnePlus phones also turn on Autostart for TnP Student.

How do updates work?

The app checks for new versions on its own. When one is out you'll get a notification; tap Update now and Android installs it over the old version, keeping your sign-in.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet — the app is Android-only for now.

I don't have a TnP account yet.

TnP accounts are created by the university for eligible students (usually from the second year). Once you have one, sign in with your college email.
